How to Play

Goal

A drowned world lies under the waves. Swim the seabed, grab relics from the wrecks and reefs, and bank them at the Beacon habitat on the lighthouse plateau. First crew member to the score target (or the highest score when time runs out) wins.

Oxygen

  • Your air drains underwater and refills at the surface or inside the Beacon habitat.
  • Run out and you drown. Deeper water is darker, so plan your route.
  • Relics are always collectable. Weapons work everywhere.

Loot

Carry up to 3. Each slows you a little. Die and you drop them. A harpoon hit hooks a relic loose.
